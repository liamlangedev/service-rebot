# UniX AI × T03 Sustainable City — Brief

**Fit: 2/5** — Supporting role only: an indoor dual-arm service humanoid that can sort waste, restock and tidy, but with no sustainability product, no deployment in this domain and no public SDK.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Best hardware / platform for this track | **Wanda 2.0** (8–16 h battery, 12 kg dual-arm payload) — or **Panther** if on site (800 mm torso lift, reaches floor level) — with **UniPicker Pro** grippers |
| Strongest proof point | **Waste sorting** shown as part of the hotel-zone demo at **CES 2026** (Jan 2026, Las Vegas). A trade-show demo, not a deployment. Nothing else in this domain |
| Fastest credible 48-hour demo | Table-top **waste / recyclables sorting station**: your own vision classifier on a laptop decides the bin, the robot's existing pick-and-place skill does the motion, a dashboard counts material streams |
| Biggest risk | **Developer access is unknown.** No public SDK, docs, GitHub or ROS 2 statement. If the booth offers pre-loaded skills only, you can orchestrate but not program |
| Pairs well with | **D-Robotics** (low-power edge classifier) · **Unitree / Genisom AI** quadrupeds (outdoor litter collection feeding the indoor sorter) · **SKYLANDX** (3D map of the building) · **Flexiv** (force-controlled disassembly / repair cell) |

## Why
- Two arms, **12 kg** dual-arm payload, **982 mm** reach and an adaptive gripper (100 N, up to 10 kg) are enough for bottles, cans, cartons, towels and amenities.
- **8–16 h** runtime (Wanda 2.0) suits long sorting or audit rounds without recharging.
- 360° LiDAR, RGB / RGBD cameras and indoor navigation allow an "energy and waste audit" patrol through hotel corridors and rooms.
- Hotel housekeeping is the company's home turf, and Leukerbad is a hotel-and-spa town: room-level waste and amenity savings are a believable local story.

## Not a fit for
- Anything **outdoors**: streets, slopes, snow, litter picking in the village — no IP rating or terrain data published.
- EV charging, environmental sensing networks, urban farming, building automation — no products or integrations.
- Teams that need a documented, self-serve API from hour zero.
