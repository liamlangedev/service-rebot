# AGIBOT — Roadmap and Strategy

⬆ [AGIBOT profile](AGIBOT.md) · [Product portfolio](AGIBOT-Products.md) · [Developer guide](AGIBOT-Developer-Guide.md) · [Solutions and projects](AGIBOT-Solutions-and-Projects.md)

> **In one paragraph:** AGIBOT (智元机器人, Zhiyuan Robotics; founded February 2023, Shanghai) went from first prototype to the **20,000th robot** in about three years and ranked **no. 1 in humanoid shipments in H1 2026** (Counterpoint: about **9,700** units, 43%+ share). Its 2026 message is "**Deployment Year One**": stop counting robots built, start counting useful work done — it even proposes a unit for that, **Worken**. The roadmap for the next six months is mostly software: GO-3 and Link-U OS open-sourcing by end of 2026, WITA-Omni interaction model reaching **Europe in January 2027**. No Swiss presence was found.

## Strategic direction and markets

### How AGIBOT describes its strategy

- **"Three intelligences in one body"** — locomotion, interaction and manipulation intelligence on one hardware platform. Products map onto it: A-series and X2 (interaction), G-series (manipulation), D-series (locomotion in the field).
- **"Deployment state"** — the claim that the industry moved in 2026 from demos to robots that work every day. AGIBOT's "XYZ curve": X = development phase (to about 2025–26), **Y = deployment growth (2026–2030)**, Z = mass adoption (after 2030). The English and Chinese texts give slightly different end years for X.
- **Outcome-based selling** — seven standard solutions (line loading, industrial handling, logistics sorting, guidance, retail station, security patrol, cleaning) sold as projects, plus rental (RaaS) and white-label ("Powered by AGIBOT").
- **Full-stack and open where it builds a moat** — own hardware, models (GO, WITA, BFM / GCFM), simulator (Genie Sim), data (AgiBot World) and platform (Genie Studio, Link-U OS). Data, simulator and older models are open; the newest production models are not yet.
- **Sequencing of markets** (from the industry report AGIBOT co-presented): first high-certainty industrial tasks (handling, sorting, inspection), then commercial services and complex industry, later everything else. **Elder care is not on the near-term list.**

## Research and open-source activity

- **Volume:** at least **18** AGIBOT-authored or co-authored papers since January 2025 (CVPR, ICLR, RSS, NeurIPS, IROS) — see [Papers and research](AGIBOT-Papers-and-Research.md). Main academic partner: OpenDriveLab at the University of Hong Kong.
- **Four research lines, each feeding a product:** real-robot data (AgiBot World → Genie Studio), VLA policies (GO-1 → GO-2 → GO-3), video world models (EnerVerse → Genie Envisioner → GE-Sim 2.0), post-deployment learning (Genie Centurion, SOP, ALOE, CEL → Genie Evolver / Genie Master).
- **GitHub (AgibotTech):** about **35 public repositories**; SDK, hand and simulator repos were updated in the week before the hackathon. Highlights: `genie_sim` (1,600+ stars), X1 open-source humanoid (three repos, 1,000–1,800 stars each), Genie Envisioner, ACoT-VLA, LIFE, `sonic_for_a3`, D1 and OmniHand SDKs.
- **Hugging Face (agibot-world):** AgiBot World 2026 shows about **2 M downloads** on the Hugging Face counter (recent-period figure); also GO-1 / GO-1 Air weights, GenieSim assets and datasets, world-model weights.
- **"AI WAVE" (22 Oct – 3 Nov 2026):** seven releases in two weeks — AGIBOT WORLD 2026 fourth batch ("Physical Agent"), G2 Max, Genie Sim, Genie Studio 2.0, Genie Master, GO-3 Preview, CEL — closed by the live convenience-store test.
- **Community building:** AgiBot World Challenge (IROS 2025; ICRA 2026, described as a **US$530,000** event), RoboColiseum public benchmark, university programme, textbook fund, OpenAtom Foundation platinum sponsorship, OpenHarmony embodied-AI committee.
- **Licence pattern:** datasets and models mostly **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** (non-commercial); Genie Sim code MPL-2.0; SDK and URDF repos BSD-3 or Mulan PSL v2. Production models (GO-2, WITA-Omni, BFM, GCFM) are closed today.
- **Gaps:** no published research on quadruped autonomy, outdoor navigation, cleaning, or elder care.

## What would impress this company at the hackathon

**Inference — not stated by AGIBOT.** Derived from what the company measures, sells and says it lacks.

| What they care about (evidence) | How to show it in 48 hours |
|---|---|
| **Sustained autonomous work, not a one-off trick** (Worken; 24-hour store trial; 64-hour factory livestream) | Run your demo **repeatedly and unattended** and report numbers: tasks completed, success rate, minutes without human touch. A 30-minute continuous run beats a single perfect take. |
| **Recovery from failure** (Genie Master, "pass / fail" grading of robots) | Build in detection and retry: the robot notices it missed, says so, and tries another way. Log the failures and show them. |
| **All three intelligences on one body** (core strategy) | Combine walking, talking and (even simple) manipulation or gesture in one flow instead of a pure chatbot or a pure motion demo. |
| **Fast site adaptation** (Genie Studio 2.0: new task site in 7 days; Chimelong live in one month) | Show how your solution was configured for Leukerbad in hours: a map, a local knowledge base, a persona — and how it would move to the next village. |
| **Multilingual, natural interaction** (WITA-Omni launching in Europe first) | German / French / English / Italian switching without a wake word; group conversation; polite turn-taking. |
| **Use of their open stack** (80,000-developer ecosystem push, open-sourcing plans) | Credit and actually use AimDK, Genie Sim, GO-1, LIFE / RoboBeats or the AgentSDK; file a useful issue or small fix. |

**By track:**

- **T01 Autonomous Urban Services (strongest fit).** AGIBOT's proof points are guides, greeters and kiosks. A Leukerbad visitor concierge on an X2 — greet, answer from a local knowledge base, walk the guest to the right door (T01.3, T01.5, T01.7) — mirrors their Kuanzhai Alley and metro deployments and adds what they do not have yet: a small alpine resort, four languages, outdoor paths. Bonus: measure it in Worken terms.
- **T02 Resilient & Safe City.** Their inspection story is quadrupeds on fixed rounds with edge / cloud compute (State Grid substation, Shangyu chemical park). A D1 patrol that detects a defined hazard or a person on the ground and escalates to a human operator (T02.2, T02.3, T02.5, T02.7) extends that line; rough-terrain autonomy on Leukerbad's slopes and stairs (T02.6) is exactly what the unreleased D2 Max is positioned for. No AGIBOT rescue product exists — a credible human-robot emergency workflow would be new to them.
- **T03 Sustainable City (weakest fit, most open).** They have sorting and handling skills and a cleaning robot, but **no waste, recycling or environmental product**. A sorting policy in Genie Sim or on a G-series arm with GO-1 (T03.1, T03.6), or a D1 that collects environmental readings on a route (T03.2, T03.7), shows a new market for existing skills. Energy use per task would fit their "output efficiency" thinking (T03.3).
- **T04 Elderly Care & Rehabilitation (greenfield).** The only healthcare deployment is a hospital guidance desk; elder care appears only as a stated wish with Malaysian partners. A companion or reminder robot on the X2 (T04.5, T04.7) built on the hospital-triage AgentSDK sample, or guided exercise with gesture mirroring (T04.3), would give them a first reference in a segment their European partner already names as a driver. Be honest about limits: X2 arms carry about 1 kg, so no lifting or transfer.
