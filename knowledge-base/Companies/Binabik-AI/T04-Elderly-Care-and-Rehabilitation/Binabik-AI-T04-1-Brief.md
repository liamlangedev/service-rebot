# Binabik AI × T04 Elderly Care & Rehabilitation — Brief

**Fit: 3/5** — A software-only "teach it once" skill layer for someone else's wheeled dual-arm robot: credible for carer-taught fetch-and-carry, but it has no care deployment and nothing is self-serve. **Without hands-on access from the Binabik team, treat it as 1/5.**

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Best hardware / platform for this track | Binabik Studio (Teach + Skill Engine) and the BDK, running on an **AgiBot G2** or **Galaxea R1 Pro** borrowed from those vendors (the only two robots Binabik names) |
| Strongest proof point | No care proof point. Closest: by **13 Feb 2026** their humanoid could "interact with people, pick and carry objects, and safely navigate shared environments" in an industrial proof of concept with an unnamed partner (Venture Kick interview) |
| Fastest credible 48-hour demo | A carer or therapist demonstrates one routine once (for example: reset a therapy station, or bring a water bottle and pill box to a bedside table); the robot repeats it with objects moved, after a human approves the plan |
| Biggest risk | Access. No public docs, SDK download or stated languages for Studio / BDK (the public GitHub org holds only side tools and forks); Studio is "request access". Everything depends on what the 8-person team hands out on site |
| Pairs well with | AGIBOT (G2) · Galaxea (R1 Pro) · D-Robotics (edge box for voice / fall trigger) · XREAL (plan preview for the carer) |

## Why
- **Teaching by one demonstration fits care work.** Carers are not programmers; "show the task once, by hand or remote control" is the right interaction model.
- **Supervised operation.** Every plan can be checked before the robot moves, which is a natural safety story next to frail people.
- **Data stays on site.** Stated for factories; the same argument works for a clinic's camera data.
- **Both named host robots are at the event**, and both are wheeled dual-arm platforms suited to indoor fetch-and-carry.
- **Healthcare is a stated interest only** 🔭: the ERF 2026 profile lists "healthcare robotics" among many sectors. No product follows from it.

## Not a fit for
- Anything with physical contact: transfers, lifting, massage, gait support, exoskeletons. No force-control or medical-safety claims exist.
- Teams that need a documented, self-serve SDK tonight.
- Companionship, voice coaching or fall detection as the core idea: Binabik offers no public speech, vision or health-monitoring modules.
