# Binabik AI × T01 Autonomous Urban Services — Brief

**Fit: 3/5** — A software-only "teach it once" skill layer for someone else's wheeled dual-arm robot: useful for the handling half of a delivery or upkeep demo, but only if the Binabik team gives you access on site (otherwise **1/5**).

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Best hardware / platform for this track | **Binabik Studio** (Teach + Skill Engine) with the **BDK**, running on a **Galaxea R1 Pro** or **AgiBot G2** (the only two robots Binabik names). Binabik has no robot of its own |
| Strongest proof point | Nothing in urban services. Closest: humanoid proof of concept with an **unnamed industrial partner** (late 2025 – early 2026, reported Feb 2026); website shows an R1 Pro loading machines and carrying parts between stations |
| Fastest credible 48-hour demo | Reception or back-of-house runner: demonstrate once how to pick a parcel or linen bundle, the robot carries it to a second station and places it. Driving comes from the robot vendor's own navigation |
| Biggest risk | **Access.** No public docs, SDK download or stated languages for Studio / BDK (the public GitHub org holds only side tools and forks); Studio is "request access". Navigation, outdoor use and people-safety are not described anywhere |
| Pairs well with | **Galaxea** (R1 Pro) · **AGIBOT** (G2) · SKYLANDX (map of the venue) · XREAL (operator view, ⚠️ unverified integration) |

## Why
- **Teach-by-demonstration fits a 48-hour clock:** one demonstration is split into steps and mapped to reusable skills, with zero-shot / few-shot generalisation claimed.
- **Both named target robots are on site**, from Galaxea and AGIBOT. That is the whole hardware story.
- **Plans can be checked before the robot moves** (supervised operation), which helps a safe public demo.
- **Carry-between-stations is already part of their machine-tending offer**, so pick, transport and place is their home ground, indoors.
- **Swiss team, small company:** direct engineer contact is likely, and it is the only way in.

## Not a fit for
- Outdoor, street, snow or stair mobility; last-mile delivery vehicles; fleet routing. Nothing public covers navigation at all.
- Teams that want a self-serve SDK, open models or datasets from minute one.
- Any robot other than R1 Pro or G2 unless Binabik confirms support in person.
