# Binabik AI × T02 Resilient & Safe City — Brief

**Fit: 2/5** — Supporting role only: a teach-by-demonstration skill layer for someone else's wheeled dual-arm robot, with no safety, inspection or rescue product and no self-serve SDK.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Best hardware / platform for this track | No own hardware. **Binabik Studio** (Teach + Skill Engine) with the **BDK**, running on an **AgiBot G2** or **Galaxea R1 Pro** (the only two publicly named platforms) |
| Strongest proof point | 2025 pre-incorporation prototype: software on a quadruped that could "patrol an area or look for changes" (reported by Venture Kick, 13 Feb 2026). Not a product today. No T02 deployment exists |
| Fastest credible 48-hour demo | Robot is shown **once** how to do an emergency manipulation in a mock plant room (close a valve, flip a breaker, swap a backup battery), then repeats it when an alarm fires |
| Biggest risk | Access. Studio is "request access" only, there are no public docs, and support for any robot other than G2 / R1 Pro is not public |
| Pairs well with | Galaxea or AGIBOT (robot body) · Unitree or Genisom AI (quadruped patrol trigger) · SKYLANDX (3D scan for change detection) · XREAL (remote operator view) |

## Why
- **Manipulation, not mobility:** the current stack is indoor pick, carry, place and machine operation. In T02 that maps to "keep infrastructure running" tasks, not to search and rescue.
- **Fast re-tasking is the resilience argument:** one demonstration to a new skill ("days" per the company; hours on site is ⚠️ unverified).
- **Both named robots are on site**, so a working pairing is plausible.
- **Stated interest, nothing more:** the ERF 2026 profile lists smart cities / infrastructure inspection and security robotics as activity areas (🔭).
- **Tiny team (8 people):** expect direct help for a few teams, not a downloadable kit.

## Not a fit for
- Outdoor, snow, rubble or steep-terrain response; quadruped or drone work.
- Mapping, SLAM, hazard sensing or digital twins as the core of the project.
- Teams that need documented APIs before committing.
