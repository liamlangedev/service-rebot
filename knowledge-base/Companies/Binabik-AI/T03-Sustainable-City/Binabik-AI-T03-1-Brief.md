# Binabik AI × T03 Sustainable City — Brief

**Fit: 2/5** — Supporting role only: a teach-by-demonstration skill layer for someone else's wheeled dual-arm robot, with no sustainability product, deployment or public SDK.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Best hardware / platform for this track | **Binabik Studio** (Teach + Skill Engine + BDK) running on a **Galaxea R1 Pro** or **AgiBot G2** from those vendors. Binabik has no robot of its own |
| Strongest proof point | None in this domain. Closest: humanoid machine-tending proof of concept with an **unnamed industrial partner** (late 2025 – early 2026, reported 13 Feb 2026) |
| Fastest credible 48-hour demo | Robot is shown **once** how to take returned reusable items or recyclables from a tray, carry them and stack / drop them by type; Binabik's stack replays it on new items |
| Biggest risk | **Access.** Studio is "request access" only, with no public docs or stated language, and no Studio / BDK code on GitHub. If the on-site team cannot onboard you in the first hours, you have nothing to build on |
| Pairs well with | Galaxea (R1 Pro), AGIBOT (G2), D-Robotics (RDK edge classifier), SKYLANDX (scan of the work cell), XREAL (operator view) |

## Why
- **Task overlap is real but narrow:** their public skills are take-from-conveyor, load, unload, transport and palletize. That maps to sorting, reuse-loop handling and crate stacking, not to monitoring, energy or outdoor work.
- **Small-data story fits a hackathon:** one demonstration instead of a dataset is exactly what a 48-hour team needs, if it works on your objects (the "up to 85%" efficiency figure is a company claim, ⚠️ unverified).
- **Both named robots are on site** (Galaxea R1 Pro, AgiBot G2), so the pairing is plausible without extra porting.
- **Gripper set matches waste items:** centric (round parts), vacuum (boxes, trays), parallel with fingertip camera. Whether these are on site is not public.
- **Swiss team, on-site engineers:** the people are the documentation.

## Not a fit for
- Environmental monitoring, building or energy automation, EV charging, outdoor litter picking, snow or green-space work.
- Teams that want a self-serve SDK, pretrained models or open-source product code (the public GitHub org holds only side tools and forks).
- Any concept on a robot other than R1 Pro or G2, unless Binabik confirms support.
