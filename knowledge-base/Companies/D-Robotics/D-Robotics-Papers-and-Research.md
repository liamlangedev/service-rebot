# D-Robotics — Papers and Research

⬆ [D-Robotics profile](D-Robotics.md) · [Solutions and projects](D-Robotics-Solutions-and-Projects.md) · [Developer guide](D-Robotics-Developer-Guide.md)

> **30-second take:** D-Robotics is not only a chip vendor. It runs an in-house research group ("D-Robotics AI Lab" plus a "Large Model Team") that has put out **16 own-led papers and 28 co-authored ones since Dec 2024**. Two of them report numbers on RDK boards and are the ones to read first: **RTPolicy** (manipulation policy at 32.9 Hz on RDK S600, about 11.6 Hz on RDK S100) and **DOSOD** (open-vocabulary detector at 47 FPS on RDK X5).

## Research footprint

- **Research-driven for a hardware company.** Separate GitHub organisation [D-Robotics-AI-Lab](https://github.com/orgs/D-Robotics-AI-Lab/repositories?type=all) (**23 repos**) next to the product organisation [D-Robotics](https://github.com/D-Robotics) (**269 repos**), and a Hugging Face organisation with **16 models and 5 datasets**. Accepted venues so far: ICRA 2025, IROS 2025 and 2026, AAAI 2026, CVPR 2026, ECCV 2026, RSS 2026.
- **Key people (from author lists):** **Wei Sui** (lead or corresponding author on almost every paper, `wei.sui@d-robotics.cc`; job title ⚠️ unverified), **Yonghao He** (project lead for manipulation policies and detection), Lingfeng Qian, Heng Zhou, Xianbao Hou, Bohao Zhang, Yusen Qin (RoboTwin), Xianda Guo (stereo and occupancy, joint with Wuhan University). Papers give the affiliation as "D-Robotics, Beijing" (one paper says Shenzhen).
- **Partner labs:** Soochow University BeeLab (Cong Yang), CASIA MAIS lab (Hu Su), HUST (Xinggang Wang), SJTU ScaleLab (Yao Mu), Tsinghua AIR / DISCOVER Robotics, University of Tokyo, and the **Horizon Robotics Robot Lab** (Liu Liu, Zhizhong Su), the parent company's group.
- **Themes:** real-time manipulation policies for edge chips, open-vocabulary detection, stereo / fisheye depth and occupancy, learned simulators and scene generation, quantization of large models for the BPU. Navigation and humanoid locomotion appear in 2026.
- **Open-source posture:** code is mostly **Apache-2.0**. Exceptions: DOSOD is **GPL-3.0**, X-Lens is **CC BY-NC 4.0** (non-commercial), Humanoid-OmniOcc data needs a signed agreement. Gap to know: the shipping stereo depth stack (`hobot_stereonet` / DStereo) and the stereo occupancy nets have **no paper**; most research results are measured on NVIDIA GPUs, not on RDK boards.
- **How this doc was researched:** web search was available but rationed (2 queries, both returned little). The paper list comes from arXiv listings by author, the arXiv HTML full text (affiliations checked paper by paper), GitHub READMEs, Hugging Face listings and Crossref DOI records. Papers not on arXiv (Chinese journals, theses) are probably missing.

## Reading list by track

### T01 Autonomous Urban Services
- [DOSOD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14680): text-prompted detection at 47 FPS on RDK X5. Lets a service or inspection robot look for "wheelchair", "parcel", "spill" without training a model.
- [X-Lens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12993): one small model for depth from fisheye + pinhole cameras; weights are public (non-commercial licence).
- [FSR-VLN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13733): a workable recipe for "guide me to X" robots (T01.5): scene graph first, VLM only at the end.
- [SkillNav](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15758): cheap fixes for VLM navigators that get stuck or loop (T01.6).
- [HoloAgent-0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23565): architecture reference for a language-driven robot agent with re-planning (code public).
- [Debris detection on RDK X5](https://doi.org/10.3390/s26144386): a full, published INT8 deployment recipe on the board most teams will get.

### T02 Resilient & Safe City
- [WM-LOCO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02542): humanoid walking on stepping stones and stairs, tested on a Unitree G1 (T02.6). Unitree robots are on site; note it ran on a Jetson, not an RDK.
- [GeoFlow-SLAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14247): SLAM that holds up on a fast-moving quadruped in texture-poor places.
- [Humanoid-OmniOcc](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22971) and [FoundationSSC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.13601): how to turn stereo cameras into a 3D occupancy map for safe movement. Use `dstereo_occnet` for the on-board version.
- [DOSOD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14680): prompt for hazards ("fire extinguisher", "person lying down", "fallen rock") on a low-power node.
- [TrustVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12571): short read on why an unaudited robot policy is a safety risk; good pitch material.

### T03 Sustainable City
- [Debris detection on RDK X5](https://doi.org/10.3390/s26144386): closest published match to litter / waste detection on this hardware (T03.1, T03.7).
- [DOSOD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14680): sort by prompt ("PET bottle", "aluminium can", "cardboard") before you have a labelled dataset (T03.1, T03.6).
- [InstaDA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02973): generate extra labelled instances when you only have a few waste photos.
- [RTPolicy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41625) or [VO-DP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15530): learned pick-and-place for a sorting arm from RGB cameras only.
- [Ridge navigation on RDK X5](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.atech.2026.101797): latency reference for a pruned YOLO11 on the X5 (T03.2).

### T04 Elderly Care & Rehabilitation
- [Service robot for the elderly and disabled on RDK-X5](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3095395): the only paper found that targets this track on this hardware; check the design before choosing yours.
- [RTPolicy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41625): the D-Robotics answer to "can a learned arm policy run on the board"; yes on S100 at about 11.6 Hz and S600 at 32.9 Hz (T04.1).
- [VO-DP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15530): one camera, 200 demos per task, pick-and-place and stacking at 87.9 % real-world success.
- [ISR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22907): clean up shaky teleoperated demos; small tool, quick win for any imitation-learning build.
- [MODEST](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14616): drinking glasses and clear bottles are the classic failure of depth cameras in care settings.
- [RDK X3 + myCobot 320 study](https://doi.org/10.1109/telecom66943.2025.11304042): mobile manipulator with re-planning on the cheapest board (T04.6).
