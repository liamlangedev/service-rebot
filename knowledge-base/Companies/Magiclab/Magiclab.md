# Magiclab Robotics

> **One-liner:** Chinese embodied-AI company (brand MagicLab, Chinese name 魔法原子 / "Magic Atom") that builds humanoids (MagicBot), quadrupeds (MagicDog), its own hands, arms and joint modules, with open BSD-licensed SDKs on GitHub.
> **Why it matters at Leukerbad:** It is one of the few vendors offering both a small programmable humanoid (Z1) and a cheap-to-risk quadruped (MagicDog EDU) with a ROS 2 SDK that already includes SLAM and navigation — a realistic 48-hour base for patrol, delivery and guide-robot ideas on steep or snowy ground.

## What they will most likely bring to the hackathon

**Best guess, not confirmed by the organisers or the company.**

- **MagicDog EDU** (most likely): small, 16 kg, the only MagicDog edition with SDK access, and the one with a ROS 2 SDK plus SLAM/navigation. Typical for developer and university programmes.
- **MagicBot Z1 Development Version** (likely): the company's show robot at CES, MWC and IFA 2026, and its most actively maintained SDK (GitHub push 29 Oct 2026). The base Z1 has **no** secondary-development access — ask which edition is on the table.
- **MagicDog-W** (possible): has its own SDK and URDF; wheels are fast on flat village streets but treat snow and ice as untested.
- **Gen1 / X1 / D1 / Y1 / T1** (less likely as hackable units): heavy, costly or newly launched; more plausible as demo units. Y1 and T1 would be the most valuable for Leukerbad's terrain if they do appear.
- First question to ask at the booth: **which editions (EDU / Development) are here, is low-level control unlocked, and is there an Ethernet port and on-board compute you may use?**

## Developer platform (summary)

- **SDKs per robot** on GitHub (`MagiclabRobotics`): `magicbot-z1_sdk`, `magicbot-gen1_sdk`, `magicdog_sdk`, `magicdog-w_sdk`, `magicdog-y1_sdk` — C++ libraries with Python bindings, mostly **BSD-3-Clause**.
- **Two control levels**: high-level commands through the built-in motion controller (gaits, postures, velocity) and **low-level per-joint control**; plus sensor streams, robot state and voice/audio.
- **ROS 2**: `magicdog_ros2_sdk` exposes devices as topics and services with **integrated SLAM and navigation**. It was last pushed in May 2025 — check it still matches current firmware. No dedicated ROS 2 repo found for the humanoids ⚠️ unverified.
- **Motion-control research path**: `magicdog_motion_sdk` (C++, PC-to-control-board over **LCM**), `magiclab_rl_lab` (RL training), `magiclab_mujoco` (MuJoCo simulation), `magiclab_deploy` (policy deployment).
- **Robot models (URDF)**: `magicbot-z1_description`, `magicbot-gen1_description`, `magicdog_description`, `magicdog-w_description`.
- **Teleoperation and imitation**: `pico_teleoperate` (PICO XR headset teleop), `magicbot-mimic` (motion imitation), `Magiclab_GMR` (motion retargeting for Z1), `magicbot-gen1_pi0_demo` (pi0 VLA demo on Gen1).
- **Models / inference**: `Inference` (multi-mode robot inference framework, Apache-2.0) and `Magic-W0` (MIT, active Oct 2026; purpose not checked ⚠️ unverified). Magic-VLA K02 weights: not public as far as found.
- **On-board compute**: 8-core CPU on all robots; AI module varies (Gen1 100 TOPS, Y1 optional 157 TOPS, X1 Orin NX / Ultra up to AGX Thor, Z1 dev optional high-compute module). Plan to run heavy models on your own laptop.
- **Practical gotchas** from the docs: the quick start needs **multicast enabled** on the network interface to the robot; the docs site may lag behind GitHub, so trust the repo headers.
- Links: [Docs centre](https://support.magiclab.top/) · [Open-source page](https://www.magiclab.top/en/opensource) · [GitHub org](https://github.com/MagiclabRobotics)
