# Unitree Robotics — Papers and Research

⬆ [Unitree Robotics profile](Unitree.md) · [Solutions and projects](Unitree-Solutions-and-Projects.md) · [Developer guide](Unitree-Developer-Guide.md)

> **How this list was built (read once):** every arXiv entry below was opened by its ID on 2026-11-14 (title, authors, date taken from arXiv; robot hardware confirmed in the abstract or full text). Unitree's own items were read from its official GitHub organisation and Hugging Face organisation. The web-search quota for this run was exhausted, so discovery relied on direct lookups of known papers and official repositories — **the third-party list is a verified selection, not an exhaustive census**. Venue is given only where arXiv or the publisher states it; otherwise "arXiv".

## Research footprint

- **Unitree (宇树科技, Hangzhou Yushu Technology) publishes almost no peer-reviewed papers.** Its research output is released as **open code, model weights and datasets** with `@misc` citations whose only author is "Unitree" — no arXiv ID, no named authors (checked in the READMEs of UnifoLM-WMA-0, UnifoLM-VLA-0, UnifoLM-WLA-1.0 and xr_teleoperate).
- **The research footprint is mostly other people's:** Unitree hardware (A1, Go1, Go2, B1 + Z1, H1, G1) is the default platform of legged-robot and humanoid learning research. Many top papers acknowledge hardware or firmware support from Unitree staff (e.g. Robot Parkour Learning, Deep Whole-Body Control, HumanPlus, Helpful DoggyBot, HOVER, CLONE), but list no Unitree co-authors.
- **Embodied-AI model line "UnifoLM"** is the in-house research programme: **WMA-0** (world-model-action, Sept 2025) → **VLA-0** (Jan 2026) → **WLA-1.0** (Sept 2026; **6B parameters**, about **2,500 h** of real-robot data, **64 tasks**) with the **UnifoLM-ER-1** embodied-reasoning VLM (4B, built on Qwen3-VL-4B).
- **Open-source posture is strong and practical:** **246 dataset repositories and 8 model repositories** on Hugging Face (counted 2026-11-14), most Apache-2.0; SDK, simulators and RL / teleoperation stacks under BSD-3 or Apache-2.0. Earlier model generations (WMA-0 code, VLA-0 weights) are **non-commercial (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)**.
- **Key people and roots:** founder and CEO **Wang Xingxing**; the company traces its technology to his **XDog** quadruped (Shanghai University, 2013–2016), which Unitree itself lists as its only "publication". Official repos openly build on academic work: Open-TeleVision (UC San Diego / MIT), Point-LIO (HKU MARS Lab), legged_gym / rsl_rl (ETH RSL), Isaac Lab (NVIDIA), LeRobot (Hugging Face).

## Reading list by track

### T01 Autonomous Urban Services
- **[Uni-NaVid](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06224)** — closest to a ready recipe for a Go2 concierge: language instructions, object search and human following from one camera, using Unitree's stock local controller.
- **[NaVILA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.04453)** — shows the split you should copy: a VLM emits "move forward 75 cm / turn left 30°" and the built-in locomotion executes it.
- **[LOVON](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06747)** — plug-and-play LLM + open-vocabulary detector on Go2 / B2; a realistic 48 h architecture for "find and go to X".
- **[WildLMa](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.15131)** and **[Helpful DoggyBot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.00231)** — fetch / deliver / tidy with a quadruped plus arm or mouth gripper.
- **[PRELUDE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.09233)** — walking among pedestrians (dynamic obstacles) with a small quadruped.
- Not a paper but directly usable: **[unitree_robotskills](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_robotskills)** (agent + SDK tools, language navigation skill).

### T02 Resilient & Safe City
- **[Agile But Safe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.17583)** — safety-switch idea for fast patrol without collisions (Go1).
- **[Commonsense Reasoning for Legged Robot Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.02666)** — VLM-in-the-loop skill selection for debris, gaps and dead ends; easy to prototype on top of high-level SDK commands.
- **[Open-TeleVision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01512)** + **[xr_teleoperate](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/xr_teleoperate)** — hazardous-environment teleoperation of a G1 from a headset, working code today.
- **[Point-LIO](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/epdf/10.1002/aisy.202200459)** + **[point_lio_unilidar](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/point_lio_unilidar)** — 3D mapping / digital twin from the Unitree L1 / L2 LiDAR.
- **[Robot Parkour Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.05665)** / **[Extreme Parkour](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.14341)** — what rubble-style terrain is feasible; do not retrain in 48 h, use the factory gait.
- **[LOVON](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06747)** — open-vocabulary target search, maps directly to "find the victim / find the hazard sign".

### T03 Sustainable City
- **[WildLMa](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.15131)** — the only verified Unitree-hardware paper here with a litter task (trash clean-up with B1 + Z1).
- **[AgriVLN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07406)** — language-guided navigation in agricultural scenes on a **Go2 Air** (the cheapest Unitree robot).
- **[UMI on Legs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10353)** and **[ODYSSEY](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.08240)** — manipulation from a Go2 with arm (pick, toss, long-horizon tasks) for litter picking or sorting.
- Unitree's own data: **[Take_Out_The_Rubbish](https://huggingface.co/datasets/unitreerobotics/G1_WBT_SMPL_Brainco_Take_Out_The_Rubbish)**, **[PickOutBatteries](https://huggingface.co/datasets/unitreerobotics/G1_Dex1_PickOutBatteries)**, **[ConveyorSorting](https://huggingface.co/datasets/unitreerobotics/G1_Dex1_ConveyorSorting)** + **[unitree_lerobot](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_lerobot)** for a G1 sorting policy.
- No directly relevant paper found for energy / building automation, EV charging or low-power edge AI on Unitree hardware.

### T04 Elderly Care & Rehabilitation
- **Guide-robot trio:** **[Kim et al. (AlienGo)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14055)**, **[DeFazio et al. (A1)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.04370)**, **[Chen et al. (Laikago)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.03927)** — harness / leash interaction, tug sensing, comfort; the best-evidenced assistive use of Unitree quadrupeds.
- **[GentleHumanoid](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.04679)** — compliant upper body for safe physical contact with people on a G1.
- **[H2-COMPACT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17627)** — humanoid as a carrying partner guided by force cues.
- **[Learning Getting-Up Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12152)** / **[HoST](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08378)** — robot fall recovery; relevant for safety arguments near frail users.
- **[Helpful DoggyBot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.00231)** — fetch-on-request in a home.
- Unitree's own data: **[Push_Wheelchair](https://huggingface.co/datasets/unitreerobotics/G1_WBT_Inspire_Push_Wheelchair)**, **[Adjust_Sickbed](https://huggingface.co/datasets/unitreerobotics/G1_WBT_Brainco_Adjust_Sickbed)**, **[Pick_Up_Medicine](https://huggingface.co/datasets/unitreerobotics/G1_WBT_Brainco_Pick_Up_Medicine)** — evidence of Unitree's planned care direction and usable for fine-tuning.
- No paper found on rehabilitation, physiotherapy, EMG or exoskeleton use of Unitree hardware.
