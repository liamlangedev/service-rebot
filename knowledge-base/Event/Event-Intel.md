# Leukerbad Robotics Hackathon 2026 — Event Intel

[Start here](../00-START-HERE.md)

> **Read this first (5 lines).**
> 1. Tracks are **open**: the organizers give a direction, the team defines its own problem, platform and approach. Expect themes, not a fixed task sheet.
> 2. Judging is **two rounds**: one champion per track (criteria: **technical quality, innovation, real-world value, completeness**), then the four champions compete for 1st / 2nd / 3rd. Winning your track is the only way into the final.
> 3. **Team size: up to 5.** Solo sign-up and on-site team formation are allowed.
> 4. Operating high-value robots yourself requires **proof of third-party liability insurance**. Without it you are limited to software, simulation, digital twins or approved lower-value devices (the site names the **LeRobot SO-101**).
> 5. Real build window is about **47–48 h**: development officially starts **Sat 13:00**, deadline is **Mon around 12:00–13:00** (sources differ, see below).

Confidence legend: no mark = stated on an organizer page · *(inference)* = my interpretation · ⚠️ unverified = could not be confirmed on a primary page.

## Format, rules and judging

### What is public (carnival website, hackathon page)

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Tracks | Four **open** challenge tracks. Organizers provide the overall direction; teams define their own problem, choose the platform and the technical approach |
| Teams | Up to 5 members; form in advance or on site; solo registration allowed |
| Eligibility | Students, engineers, researchers, developers, enthusiasts, entrepreneurs. No robotics experience required |
| Physical robot required? | **No.** On-site robots are encouraged, but software, AI, simulation and digital-twin projects are "equally welcome" |
| Own equipment | Not needed; you may bring tools and materials if they comply with on-site safety rules |
| Mentors | Technical mentors available on site; level of support depends on mentor and equipment arrangements |
| Device access | Organizer decides per device, based on equipment management, safety and **insurance status**. Some devices need a professional technician present |
| Insurance | Mandatory **third-party liability insurance** to personally operate high-value devices. It must explicitly cover accidental damage to borrowed or entrusted third-party equipment during robotics development and testing. Ordinary personal liability cover may not be enough |
| No insurance | Still allowed: software, AI algorithms, simulation, digital twins, or organizer-approved lower-value devices such as the **LeRobot SO-101** |
| Damage | Participants may be held responsible for damage they cause, under applicable law and the participation rules |
| Final say | Award amounts, eligibility and conditions are subject to the official rules published by the organizer |

### Judging (carnival website)

| Round | Who | Criteria / outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Round 1 — track winners | All projects, judged inside their track | **Technical quality · innovation · real-world value · completeness**. One champion per track |
| Round 2 — grand final | The four track champions | Jury picks Grand Champion (1st), Runner-up (2nd), Third Place |
| Format of the final | Team pitch plus **live robot demonstration**, expert and jury evaluation | |
| Cash prize wording | Teams that excel in **innovation, technical implementation, real-world value and presentation** | |

- Weighting of the criteria is **not published**. ⚠️
- *(inference)* With four criteria and a live-demo final, a **complete, working, locally grounded demo** beats an ambitious half-working one. "Completeness" is an explicit criterion, which is unusual and worth planning for.
- *(inference)* Because only track champions reach the final, **track choice is a competition-density decision** as much as a topic decision. Ask at kickoff how many teams are in each track.

**What this predicts for Leukerbad** *(all inference)*:
- Live demo is decisive; slides alone will not carry a team.
- One new capability shown clearly on real hardware (RoBoost pattern) beats a broad platform story.
- Measurable results are remembered (the 8-second lap). Bring a number.
- Expect about 30 to 40 percent no-shows against the registered count, so the real field may be well under 220.
- EIII wants follow-on projects. A credible "what we do in the next three months" slide fits the lab-access prize.

## Leukerbad in numbers — hooks for locally relevant projects

### Baseline

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Residents | **1,329** (Dec 2020) | Wikipedia |
| Elevation / area | 1,411 m / 67.2 km² | Wikipedia |
| Overnight stays | **750,878** in 2024/25 (+1.2 %); 660,430 in 2020/21 with **92.3 % Swiss guests** | My Leukerbad AG annual reports (via search snippet ⚠️ PDFs not opened) |
| Thermal water | About **3.9 million litres per day**, up to **51 °C**, **65 springs** (8 most productive used ⚠️ snippet) | Valais tourism, Wikipedia |
| Thermal pools | 30 per the tourism organization, 22 per Wikipedia ⚠️ sources differ | |
| Access | Road only, from Leuk in the Rhône valley; the railway closed in 1967 | Wikipedia |
| Economy | Hotels and restaurants were **49.4 %** of service-sector jobs (2008) | Wikipedia |
| Municipal finances | First Swiss municipality to go bankrupt: **CHF 346 million** debt by 1998, under cantonal administration 1998–2004 | Wikipedia |

- *(inference)* Residents-to-guests ratio: about 750,000 overnight stays for about 1,300 residents means services are sized for visitors, with strong seasonal peaks and a small local workforce. This is the core argument for automation in every track.

### T01 Autonomous Urban Services

- Village is compact but steep, with snow and ice in winter. *(inference)* Slopes, stairs and wet floors are the real navigation test, more than distance.
- The carnival already places robots in hotels, restaurants, a supermarket, the tourist centre and the town square. *(inference)* A project that plugs into one of these venues has built-in users and a demo site.
- Guest mix is over 90 % Swiss but the tourist centre KPI is "multilingual performance". Languages: German-speaking village (82.8 % German in 2000) in a bilingual canton.
- Labour: half of service jobs are hospitality. *(inference)* Luggage, linen, towels and room-service logistics between hotels and baths are plausible delivery use cases.

### T02 Resilient & Safe City

- Avalanches destroyed parts of the village several times between the 16th and 18th centuries (Wikipedia).
- Modern closures: access roads via Inden and Albinen have been closed for avalanche danger, with about **10,000 tourists** reported stuck in one episode (regional news, date ⚠️ unverified).
- **Gazex** remote avalanche-release installations on the Gemmi, triggered by radio from the village, are checked before each winter (regional news, via snippet).
- Single road access and no rail. *(inference)* Isolation scenarios (supply, evacuation information, medical logistics) are locally credible.
- Cable cars: Gemmi (built 1957, to 2,322 m) and Torrent (1970–72). *(inference)* Inspection of lift infrastructure, protection structures and thermal-water pipes fits quadrupeds and handheld LiDAR.

### T03 Sustainable City

- Thermal water at up to 51 °C heats ordinary water through a heat exchanger for several hotels and baths (Valais tourism).
- Waste thermal water from the Lorenz bath is routed under steep parking-garage ramps to keep them free of snow and ice (Valais tourism).
- *(inference)* Hooks: monitoring heat loss in pipes and pools with thermal imaging, leak detection, pool water quality sampling, optimising energy recovery, waste sorting for hotel and restaurant volumes that swing with occupancy.
- Claims about the share of the village heated this way or CO2 savings could not be confirmed. ⚠️

### T04 Elderly Care & Rehabilitation

- **Leukerbad Clinic** (Rehazentrum), Willy-Spühlerstrasse 2: rehabilitation centre since **1961**, focused on musculoskeletal rehabilitation, plus oncology and internal medicine; thermal water is part of therapy. Neurological rehabilitation is mentioned by one regional listing only ⚠️.
- **943 inpatient discharges in 2019** (third-party directory, ⚠️ old and not from the clinic). Bed count not found.
- Share of residents over 64 was 9.4 % in 2000 (Wikipedia, outdated). *(inference)* The elderly population that matters here is mostly **guests and patients**, not residents.
- *(inference)* Hooks: sit-to-stand and gait support, exercise coaching with pose or EMG feedback, fall detection on wet spa floors, pool-entry assistance, fetching and carrying for people on crutches, companionship in several languages.
- The carnival description lists hospitals among deployment places (Luma). *(inference)* Ask whether clinic staff are available as test users or advisors.

## Open questions to resolve at kickoff

- [ ] Exact **submission deadline** (12:00 or 13:00 on Monday) and what must be submitted (repo, video, slides, form)?
- [ ] **Pitch format**: minutes for pitch, demo and Q&A in round 1 and in the final? Is a backup video allowed if the robot fails?
- [ ] **Criteria weighting** for technical quality, innovation, real-world value, completeness? Is presentation scored in round 1?
- [ ] How many teams per track, and can a team **switch track** after kickoff?
- [ ] Is each track judged by a separate panel? Who are the final-round judges?
- [ ] **Prize split**: cash per place, per-track prizes, how much of the CHF 50,000 is in-kind?
- [ ] **Hardware list with models and unit counts**. How is hardware allocated at 11:00: first come, lottery, by proposal?
- [ ] Which devices require **insurance proof**, what document is accepted, and can it still be arranged on site?
- [ ] Which devices need a vendor technician present, and during which hours?
- [ ] Vendor **SDK, documentation and on-site engineers** per platform? Any vendor bounties or special prizes?
- [ ] May robots **leave the Sportarena** for tests in the village, clinic, baths or outdoors?
- [ ] Network: Wi-Fi quality, wired ports, 5G; are cloud APIs and hosted models allowed?
- [ ] On-site **compute**: GPUs, edge boards, cloud credits?
- [ ] Rules on **pre-existing code**, open-source models and prior work?
- [ ] **IP** ownership and any publication or open-source requirement?
- [ ] Sportarena **overnight access** and quiet hours in the sleeping area?
- [ ] Access to **real users or domain experts** (clinic, municipality, tourism office, hotels) during the build?
- [ ] Which opening room is correct for Saturday morning (town hall council chamber or school conference hall)?
- [ ] Eligibility conditions for the China trip and the three-month lab access?
